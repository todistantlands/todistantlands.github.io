Just wanted to post a quick shout-out to recent Bloggies-winner [elmcat](https://elmc.at/) for setting up [rootring](https://rootr.ing/), an old-internet styled webring for RPG blogs.

In the wasteland of the post-Web 2.0 internet I'm grateful for anything that helps me discover new RPG blogs without having to use a social platform. The corporate-owned web is a nightmare and the walls close in a little more every day. The more we can find each other and share our work through personal projects like this, the better off we all are.

You can read more about rootring on [elmcat's announcement post](https://elmc.at/rootring-is-live/). As you can see at the bottom of the page, this blog is already part of the ring. You should join too.
